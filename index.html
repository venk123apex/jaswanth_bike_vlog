Top 10 Motorcycles

1. Yamaha YZF-R1

Engine: 998cc crossplane inline-4
Power: 200 hp
Specs: MotoGP-derived electronics package, titanium connecting rods, and unmatched track handling precision.

2. Kawasaki Ninja H2

Engine: 998cc supercharged inline-4
Power: 228+ hp
Specs: Features an in-house developed centrifugal supercharger delivering ferocious acceleration and top-tier hyper-sport engineering.

3. Ducati Panigale V4

Engine: 1,103cc Desmosedici Stradale V4
Power: 214 hp
Specs: Counter-rotating crankshaft, dry weight optimization, and razor-sharp Italian superbike aerodynamics.

4. Honda CBR1000RR-R Fireblade

Engine: 1000cc inline-4
Power: 214 hp
Specs: Built with direct input from HRC racing programs, winglets for high-speed stability, and an advanced IMU.

5. BMW S1000RR

Engine: 999cc inline-4 with ShiftCam
Power: 205 hp
Specs: ShiftCam variable valve timing technology broadens the powerband for massive low-end torque and screaming top-end output.

6. Suzuki Hayabusa

Engine: 1,340cc inline-4
Power: 187 hp
Specs: Legendary status for straight-line stability, wind-cheating bodywork, and bulletproof reliability.

7. KTM 1290 Super Duke R

Engine: 1,301cc LC8 V-twin
Power: 180 hp
Specs: Known as the "Beast," it combines naked-bike ergonomics with brutal torque and track-capable agility.

8. Triumph Bonneville T120

Engine: 1,200cc high-torque parallel-twin
Power: 79 hp
Specs: Timeless classic British styling paired with modern safety electronics, ABS, and ride-by-wire throttles.

9. Harley-Davidson Fat Boy

Engine: Milwaukee-Eight 114 V-twin
Power: 94 hp (Torque: 155 Nm)
Specs: Iconic muscular silhouette, solid Lakester wheels, and a relaxed, low-slung cruising geometry.

10. BMW R 1250 GS Adventure

Engine: 1,254cc shift-cam boxer twin
Power: 136 hp
Specs: The ultimate long-distance adventure tourer equipped with massive fuel range, electronic suspension, and rugged off-road geometry.
